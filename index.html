I’m a final-year IT student, full-stack builder and founder. I live where AI, data and product meet, and I’m drawn to one question: how do you turn a messy problem into a tool people reach for?

I got hooked on code by wondering how websites actually work. Today, I build with React, ASP.NET Core, SQL and Azure, using AI to research, prototype and design faster. I care as much about how a product feels as how it works.
